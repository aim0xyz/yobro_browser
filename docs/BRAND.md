# YoBro brand direction

## Name system

- Product name: **YoBro**
- Spoken name and activation phrase: **“Yo Bro”**
- Name idea: **YO = Your**, **BRO = Browser**, and “bro” is the helpful companion at your side.
- Brand signature: **Your browser. Your bro.**
- Benefit-led slogan: **The browser that has your back.**

Use **YoBro** in product names, app listings and running text. Use **Yo Bro** only when addressing the assistant or demonstrating a prompt.

## Domain direction

Live RDAP checks on 2026-09-06 found `yobro.com`, `yobro.ai` and `yobro.app` registered. At that moment, the following `.com` names returned no registry record:

1. `getyobro.com` — best launch and consumer brand domain
2. `withyobro.com` — warm and collaborative
3. `tryyobro.com` — strong for campaigns, less ideal as the permanent home
4. `yobrobrowser.com` — clearest fallback, but longer
5. `browseyobro.com` — descriptive fallback

Domain status can change at any time. Registration availability and trademarks must be checked again immediately before purchase.

## Business model

### Free

Local browser, profiles, spaces, imports, privacy tools and basic BYOK assistant access. This creates distribution and keeps the privacy promise credible.

### YoBro Pro

Target: **€12/month or €99/year**. Charge for outcomes rather than tabs: encrypted cross-device sync, advanced AI workflows, reusable actions, mail assistance, scheduled tasks and priority updates. Managed AI usage should have a clear monthly allowance; additional usage should be metered so heavy users cannot destroy the margin.

### YoBro Teams

Target: **€24/user/month**. Shared spaces, team knowledge, role controls, managed providers, audit history and administrative policies. This is likely the strongest long-term margin because businesses pay for control and collaboration, not merely another browser.

### Launch revenue

Offer a limited **Founding Bro** license around **€59–79** for early adopters. It can finance development and create a committed community, but it must not promise unlimited lifetime cloud AI or sync costs.

## Monetization principles

- Do not sell browsing history or personal data.
- Do not hide affiliate rankings inside AI answers.
- Keep search partnerships optional and visibly disclosed.
- Make approval explicit before purchases, messages or account-changing actions.
- Treat marketplace revenue and partner commissions as secondary; subscriptions should be the core business.
