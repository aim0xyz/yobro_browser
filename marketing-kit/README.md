## Launch post — LinkedIn

Browsers were designed for opening pages. Our work now involves comparing, researching, writing and collaborating with AI across dozens of tabs.

That is why I’m building **YOBRO**, a native macOS browser with a calmer model for modern work:

- Spaces keep projects separate.
- Split view puts related pages side by side.
- Agents work in a dedicated pane without taking over your active tab.
- Access can be paused visibly at any time.
- Local profiles separate cookies, history and website data.

YOBRO is built with SwiftUI and Apple WebKit — no Electron shell.

**The browser that has your back.**

## Claims guardrail

Use “prototype”, “preview” or “early access” until the public release is production-ready. Avoid claiming autonomous purchases, message sending, full Chrome-extension compatibility or cross-device sync. Do not show real inboxes, browsing history, account names, API keys or production URLs in promotional captures.
